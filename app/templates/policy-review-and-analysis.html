WHY POLICY INTELLIGENCE

Policy decisions require evidence and context.

Policies operate within complex social, economic, political and institutional environments. Their effectiveness depends not only on what is written, but also on implementation realities, stakeholder interests and the consequences of policy choices.

We combine document review, policy research, stakeholder evidence and analytical assessment to clarify what is working, what needs attention and what options are available.
