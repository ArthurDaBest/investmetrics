Begarving Arthur

Field Research and Data Expert

Begarving Mwakapala Arthur is a seasoned data, research, and business development consultant with extensive experience in the Tanzanian market. As the founder of Investmetrics, he leads a team dedicated to helping businesses and organizations make data-driven decisions to optimize investments and policies. With expertise in quantitative and qualitative research, business development, and data visualization, Begarving has been instrumental in driving systems-wide changes for sustainable impact.

Holding a BSc in Economic Policy and Planning from Mzumbe University, Begarving is currently pursuing a MicroMasters in Data, Economics, and Development Policy from MIT. His technical skills include proficiency in statistical research tools like SPSS, STATA, Python, Smart Pls, IBM AMOS, and advanced data collection platforms such as Kobo Toolbox and Google Suite. He is also a published author in several peer-reviewed journals, contributing to research on entrepreneurship, employee performance, and procurement systems in Tanzania.

Begarving has successfully led numerous consultancy projects, including market assessments, baseline studies, and evaluations for sectors ranging from agriculture to local government authorities. His vision for Investmetrics is to provide businesses with the insights they need to thrive in a rapidly evolving economy, leveraging innovative data solutions to achieve measurable results.

  • Policy Analysis & Evaluation
  • Monitoring and Evaluation

  • Data Collection, Management & Analysis
  • Data Visualization
