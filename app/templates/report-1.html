REPORT OVERVIEW

Understanding qualitative research approaches.

Qualitative research methods are used to explore human experiences, social phenomena and cultural contexts in depth. They can provide contextual and experiential insights that are difficult to capture through numerical measures alone.

This report provides a comparative reference to major qualitative models and methods, showing their primary use, supported data types, selection considerations and commonly used software.

QUALITATIVE RESEARCH

Table of Qualitative Models.

The table compares commonly used qualitative approaches by their primary purpose, relevant evidence, appropriate applications, selection considerations and supporting software.

Qualitative Model / Method Category Primary Use Data Types Supported Best For Criteria to Choose Tools / Software
Grounded Theory Qualitative Research Develops theories based on data collected during the study. Interview transcripts, observational data Exploring processes, actions or interactions When no pre-existing theory adequately explains the phenomenon NVivo, Atlas.ti, Dedoose
Phenomenology Qualitative Research Explores lived experiences of individuals Personal interviews, focus groups Understanding personal perceptions and emotions When seeking to understand the essence of experiences MAXQDA, NVivo
Ethnography Qualitative Research Studies cultural phenomena within a specific group Field notes, participant observations Exploring cultural or social dynamics When studying people in their natural environments Atlas.ti, NVivo
Narrative Analysis Qualitative Research Examines personal stories to explore meanings Interviews, autobiographies, biographies Understanding how individuals construct meaning through stories When studying stories or narratives as data Atlas.ti, MAXQDA
Case Study Qualitative Research In-depth analysis of a specific case or event Documents, interviews, field notes Detailed understanding of a single case When focusing on one specific case or a small set of cases NVivo, MAXQDA
Thematic Analysis Qualitative Research Identifies and analyses patterns or themes in data Interview transcripts, open-ended surveys Exploring common themes across qualitative data When searching for recurring patterns or themes in data NVivo, Atlas.ti
Content Analysis Qualitative Research Analyses textual or visual content Social media posts, documents, audiovisual materials Understanding frequencies or trends in content When analysing large sets of qualitative data systematically Atlas.ti, NVivo, Leximancer
Discourse Analysis Qualitative Research Studies language use in communication Speeches, conversations, written text Examining the role of language in social and cultural contexts When studying language use or communication patterns Atlas.ti, NVivo
Framework Analysis Qualitative Research Analyses data using a structured framework Interviews, focus groups, documents Policy research, programme evaluations When applying a pre-defined analytical framework NVivo, MAXQDA
Participatory Action Research (PAR) Qualitative Research Collaborates with participants to solve problems Workshops, focus groups Action-oriented research for social change When involving participants actively in the research process NVivo, Atlas.ti
Focus Group Analysis Qualitative Research Collects group perspectives on a specific topic Focus group discussions Exploring group dynamics and shared experiences When studying group interactions and shared opinions NVivo, Atlas.ti, MAXQDA
Observation Analysis Qualitative Research Analyses behaviours and interactions in natural settings Field observations, videos Understanding behaviour in real-world contexts When observing participants in natural settings Atlas.ti, NVivo

Method selection should be guided by the research question, study purpose, type of evidence available and the analytical depth required.

02
ANALYTICAL TOOLS

Key Tools for Qualitative Analysis.

Qualitative data analysis tools can support the organisation, coding, interpretation and visualisation of unstructured data such as interview transcripts, focus-group discussions, documents, audio, video and observational material.

The table below summarises commonly used tools and the types of qualitative research tasks for which they can be applied.

Tool Purpose Features Best For
NVivo Comprehensive qualitative data analysis Thematic coding, multimedia support, data visualisation Large projects, team-based research
Atlas.ti Advanced qualitative analysis Code networks, auto-coding, relationship diagrams Academics, ethnography, case studies
MAXQDA Mixed-methods and qualitative research Coding browser, integration with SPSS, visualisation tools Mixed-methods projects, multilingual data
Dedoose Cloud-based mixed-methods analysis Real-time collaboration, affordable pricing Small research teams, mixed data sources
Quirkos Beginner-friendly qualitative analysis Visual drag-and-drop interface Small-scale projects, new researchers
Leximancer Automated text analysis Concept mapping, theme extraction, sentiment analysis Large textual datasets, thematic analysis
HyperRESEARCH Flexible qualitative and mixed-methods analysis Supports text, audio and video analysis; SPSS integration Mixed-methods research, multimedia data
QDA Miner Text and image analysis Content analysis, word frequencies, statistical graphics Researchers working with large datasets
Transana Video and audio qualitative analysis Segmentation, coding and visual timeline analysis Projects focusing on video/audio interviews
CAQDAS Tools (Framework) Supports qualitative data organisation and analysis Coding, retrieval and visualisation of data General-purpose qualitative analysis
PUBLICATION NOTE

A practical research reference.

This publication is intended as a comparative reference for researchers considering different qualitative approaches and analytical tools. Methodological decisions should ultimately follow the research question, research design and evidence requirements of the individual study.
