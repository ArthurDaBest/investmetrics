HOW WE MENTOR

Research capability grows through guided practice.

Strong research requires more than knowing terminology. Researchers need to understand why particular methodological decisions are appropriate and how those decisions affect the quality of evidence produced.

Our mentorship model combines explanation, practical application, critical review and progressive improvement so participants can develop capability they can continue using independently.

WHO WE SUPPORT

Learning designed around different research journeys.

The level and intensity of support can be adapted according to academic stage, research experience and professional objectives.

01

Students

Structured support for developing research understanding, proposals, methodology and analytical confidence.

02

Researchers

Methodological and analytical guidance for strengthening research design, analysis and scholarly outputs.

03

Professional Teams

Research-method and analytical capacity building for organisations seeking stronger internal evidence capability.

LEARNING JOURNEY

From research idea to credible findings.

Participants progressively develop the ability to frame research, select appropriate methods, generate evidence, analyse findings and communicate results.

01

Frame

Develop clear topics, questions, objectives and research purpose.

02

Design

Select suitable research designs, sampling and methods.

03

Collect

Apply appropriate tools for generating reliable research data.

04

Analyse

Apply quantitative and qualitative analytical techniques.

05

Communicate

Interpret findings and communicate conclusions clearly.

CORE LEARNING AREAS

Building research capability from the foundation upward.

Learning areas can be delivered as focused mentorship, structured training or combined into a progressive research capability programme.

01
RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Research Fundamentals

Build understanding of research purpose, topic development, research questions, scientific inquiry, research ethics and academic integrity.

Research purpose Topics Research questions Research ethics
02
METHODOLOGY

Research Design & Methods

Understand research design, sampling, data collection and the methodological choices needed to align evidence generation with research objectives.

Research design Sampling Data collection Method selection
03
RESEARCH DEVELOPMENT

Proposal Development

Develop coherent research titles, objectives and proposals that clearly explain the study problem, methodology, anticipated outcomes and research logic.

Research titles Objectives Proposal structure Method justification
04
EVIDENCE GENERATION

Data Collection Techniques

Develop the ability to select and use appropriate research instruments and data-collection approaches according to the study design.

Surveys Interviews Observation Research tools
05
ANALYTICAL CAPABILITY

Quantitative Analysis

Strengthen understanding of descriptive and inferential techniques, interpretation of numerical evidence and the practical use of analytical software.

Descriptive analysis Inferential analysis Interpretation Statistical software
06
QUALITATIVE INTELLIGENCE

Qualitative Analysis

Develop practical understanding of qualitative analysis, including coding, content analysis, thematic interpretation and communication of qualitative findings.

Coding Content analysis Thematic analysis Interpretation
