WHO WE ARE

From information to usable intelligence.

Infometrics Solution Limited, operating under the brand Investmetrics, provides research, data analytics, advisory, professional learning and intelligence solutions.

The Investmetrics brand represents the professional and operational face of Infometrics Solution Limited. Our work brings together research methodology, data systems, analytical expertise, technology and implementation thinking to help organisations make stronger decisions.

We work across public, private, development and academic environments, supporting assignments that require credible evidence, disciplined analysis and practical decision support.
