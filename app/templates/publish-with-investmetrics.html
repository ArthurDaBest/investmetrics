Before Submission

Prepare your IJIRI manuscript carefully.

Download and use the official IJIRI resources above. Before submission, authors should ensure that the manuscript satisfies the journal's core preparation requirements.

Author Information

Provide complete author names and affiliations, identify the corresponding author, and include a valid email address. ORCID should be provided where available.

Clear Abstract or Summary

Keep the paper title within 15 words and the optional subtitle within 10 words. The abstract should not exceed 300 words. Include 3–5 distinct, relevant keywords.

Transparent Methodology

Explain how evidence was generated, analysed and interpreted so that readers can understand the basis of the conclusions.

Evidence-Based Findings

Present results clearly using appropriate narrative, tables, figures or analytical visualisations.

References & Attribution

Use APA 7th Edition consistently for in-text citations and the reference list. Every citation must have a matching reference entry and vice versa.

Research Declarations

Include relevant ethics, funding, conflict-of-interest, data-use, authorship and AI-use declarations. Similarity should be below 18% and AI-generated content below 15%, with AI use acknowledged where applicable.

IJIRI Editorial Journey

From submission to publication.

IJIRI manuscripts move through a structured editorial and review process. The stated periods are operational targets and may vary with manuscript complexity, reviewer availability and the extent of revision required.

01

Initial Submission

The corresponding author submits the manuscript, author information and required declarations.

02

Editorial Check

Initial screening for completeness, scope, formatting and submission compliance — within 24 hours.

03

Deep Review

Editorial assessment of research quality, originality, relevance and readiness — 2–3 business days.

04

Decision Communicated

The editorial decision and required next action are communicated — normally within 4–6 business days.

05

Peer Review

Manuscripts proceeding to external review are assessed by relevant reviewers — target about 2 working days.

06

Revision & Approval

Authors respond to review comments and the revised manuscript is assessed — target about 3 working days.

07

Galley Proof

Final proofreading, formatting and publication preparation — target about 1 working day.

08

Publication

Approved articles are published online and included in applicable journal indexing or archiving arrangements.

Originality

Submitted work should represent genuine intellectual or analytical contribution.

Methodological Clarity

Methods and evidence should be reported sufficiently clearly for the intended audience.

Responsible Attribution

Sources, authorship and intellectual contributions should be acknowledged appropriately.

Publication Readiness

Manuscripts should be coherent, complete and presented to a professional standard.

Publication Standards

Quality begins before publication.

IJIRI publication resources are designed to strengthen clarity, consistency and scholarly presentation. The Author Guidelines and Manuscript Template provide structure, while responsibility for accuracy, originality, ethical conduct and appropriate attribution remains with the authors.
